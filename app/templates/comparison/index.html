Where the Traditional Method Excels
  • Deterministic Constraint Guarantees: Strictly enforces predefined weight distributions and greedy bipartite match criteria.
  • Faster Execution on Small Cohorts: Runs in single-digit milliseconds without requiring distance computation or iterative convergence.
  • Predictability: Clear arithmetic formula makes it straightforward to explain exact point assignments to external reviewers.
Where AI Clustering Excels
  • Multi-Skill Archetype Discovery: Discovers natural combinations of skills (e.g., students who pair React with UI design vs Backend with Cloud) that rigid rules miss.
  • Balanced Role Distribution: Pulls from orthogonal clusters to assemble multifaceted teams with high functional diversity.
  • Scalability: Adapts automatically as new students join without requiring manual re-tuning of five separate arbitrary weight coefficients.
