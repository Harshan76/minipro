How AI-Powered Recommendations Work

The AI model discovers patterns in student skills and interests. These patterns help the team builder recommend groups with complementary strengths. The final assignments are checked against project requirements.

Scientific Integrity Note: K-Means clustering discovers natural groupings and archetypes within student skill profiles; it does not predict team success or grades. Machine learning pattern discovery is cleanly decoupled from the constraint-satisfaction assignment algorithm that guarantees project requirements.
